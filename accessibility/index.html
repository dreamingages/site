Accessibility

Dreaming Ages is meant to be easy to read and use at the end of a long day, including for people who see, hear, move or think differently. We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, on every page of dreamingages.com.

When and how we tested

Last tested: 25 October 2026, on the home page, the episode pages, the free companion pages, the reading lists, the sleep guide and quiz, and the privacy and terms pages.

  • Automated checks with the axe-core testing engine.
  • Measured text contrast against the WCAG minimums.
  • Keyboard-only use, including a skip link and a visible focus outline.
  • Pages at 200% and 400% zoom, at phone width, and with wider text spacing.
  • The reduced-motion setting.

This is our own testing. It is not a certificate or an independent audit, and we have not yet tested with people who use screen readers every day.

What we do

  • Body text is 18 px or larger, with line lengths of about 70 characters and high-contrast colours on a dark background.
  • The star background stays out of the text, and it turns off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Every page has a skip link, headings in order and one main area.
  • Form fields have visible labels.
  • The pages respond to the high-contrast setting on your device.

What we know still needs work

  • Episode pages do not yet have full transcripts. Our videos play on YouTube, which provides its own captions and player controls.
  • The printable companion PDFs have not been fully checked for screen readers. If one does not work for you, write to us and we will send another format.
  • The YouTube player and the email signup form are provided by other companies, so we control only how they are labelled and placed.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to read or use, email hello@dreamingages.com with the page address and what happened. We will fix it, or offer another way to get the same content.

This statement describes the site as tested on the date above. We update it when we retest.
