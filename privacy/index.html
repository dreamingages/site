Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The short version.

  • This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Videos are click-to-play. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.
  • If you sign up for the free companions, your email goes to Kit, the service that sends them. Unsubscribe any time with one click.

dreamingages.com is run by the Dreaming Ages channel owner ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you visit the site, sign up for the free companions, or follow a link from it. Questions or requests: hello@dreamingages.com.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use browser storage. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, so there is nothing to accept or decline when you arrive. The sleep quiz runs entirely in your browser, and your answers never leave the page.

Videos (YouTube)

Each video starts as a still picture hosted on this site. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From then on, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may set cookies or use similar storage on your device, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. If you'd rather not, use the "Watch on YouTube" link, or don't press play.

Free companions by email (Kit)

When you submit the sign-up form, your email address, and your first name if you give it, are sent to Kit (kit.com), the email service we use to send the companion library and an occasional bedtime email. We use them for nothing else, and we never sell your email or share it for advertising. Kit's own pages may set their own cookies. Kit may store your details on servers outside your country. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to YouTube, YouTube Music, Buy Me a Coffee and Amazon. Nothing from these sites loads until you click. Once you're there, their own privacy and cookie policies apply.

  • Amazon: As an Amazon Associate, Dreaming Ages earns from qualifying purchases. If you follow a book or product link, Amazon may set a cookie that records that you came from this site, so we can earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: if you choose to support us there, Buy Me a Coffee and its payment provider handle the payment. We never see your card details.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as GitHub explains here. GitHub Pages does not set cookies on this site.

Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and this site works fully without them. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, Canada or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, or to object to how it's used. Email hello@dreamingages.com and we'll help. You can also contact your local data protection authority. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If this page changes, the date at the top will change too.

Dreaming Ages History Uploader

Dreaming Ages History Uploader is a private tool used only by the Dreaming Ages channel owner, who owns the YouTube channels Dreaming Ages History and The Big Mysteries, to manage those channels. It uploads videos, edits titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and channel settings, posts the owner's own comments, and reads channel statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, through YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission. It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other person, viewer or YouTube user.

How data is used

Data from the owner's YouTube account is used only to manage the owner's own channels. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth access tokens are stored only on a private computer controlled by the owner, in files that only the owner's account can read. Channel metadata and statistics are used only to manage the owner's channels and are not kept beyond what that requires.

Data deletion and revoking access

  • You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the locally stored token files removes all stored credentials straight away. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the account.
  • To request deletion of any data, or with any questions, email hello@dreamingages.com.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
