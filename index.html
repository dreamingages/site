What is Dreaming Ages?

Dreaming Ages is calm history for sleep. Each episode is a long, slow journey through one place and time, from the Nile and the Silk Road to Viking fjords, feudal Japan and the old Gaelic festival of Samhain, narrated softly with quiet music and slow images. There are no cliff-hangers, no loud sounds and no mid-video ads, so you can press play, dim the screen and let it run.

The history is real. Every script is researched from published books, fact-checked, and honest about where historians disagree. You can read how we make the episodes, find books for each episode in our reading lists, or subscribe on YouTube for three new journeys a week.

Prefer a podcast app? Dreaming Ages History is also on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

Narration on the channel is AI-generated text-to-speech. Not medical advice: if poor sleep lasts or affects your days, please talk to a doctor. Questions: hello@dreamingages.com
